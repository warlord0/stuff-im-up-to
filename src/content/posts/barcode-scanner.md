I found this very useful. Not just scanning QR codes, but I can use Vivaldi to scan any barcode and copy the output to the clipboard.

Keep an eye on the address/URL line in the browser. At the end of it is a small QR scanning icon. It's not just for QR codes.

https://help.vivaldi.com/android/android-tools/scan-qr-codes/
