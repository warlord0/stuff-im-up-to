In the world of Office 365 I find myself using a crippled version of Visio. Any shapes I want are part of an upgrade package, so that's not going to happen. Most of the shapes and Icons I want to use aren't available to me, so Visio is worthless.

Back to Open Source, LibreOffice Draw has some great libraries for shapes. One of the best I found is from [VRT Systems](https://www.vrt.com.au/downloads/vrt-network-equipment). Download and launch the file in LibreOffice, and it installs as an extension. Show the gallery on the right-hand toolbar, and I am back to being able to draw some very tidy looking graphics, none of the boring boxes in Visio.

![VRT Systems Network Shapes](/blog-media/2024/01/drawing4.png)

But sharing with others in the M\$ world means Visio is the tool we should use. My workaround, work in LibreOffice, right-click the shape in Draw and convert it to meta, copy it and paste it into Visio. It may not have all the handles in places I'd like, but it's a vast improvement over the limited Visio shapes.
