I've seen a few user complain that Thunderbird isn't exactly pretty as a mail client, especially those coming from Outlook. There's a few addons you can use to really change that.

The ones I've chosen all come from one developer [Dillinger](https://addons.thunderbird.net/en-US/thunderbird/user/dillinger/).

[Numix Blue (dark)](https://addons.thunderbird.net/en-US/thunderbird/addon/numix-blue-dark)

[Phoenity Buttons](https://addons.thunderbird.net/en-US/thunderbird/addon/phoenity-buttons/)

[Phoenity Icons](https://addons.thunderbird.net/en-US/thunderbird/addon/phoenity-icons/)
