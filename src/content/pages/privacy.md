A short one, because there isn't much to say. This site doesn't collect anything from you.

## What is collected

This site is statically generated and sets no cookies or trackers of its own. There's no analytics, no newsletter signup, and no comment or contact form, so nothing you do here gets submitted anywhere.

## Your reading preference

The light or dark mode you pick is stored in your browser's local storage under `theme`. It never leaves your device, and you can clear it at any time.

## Fonts

Body and code text are loaded from [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/). Your browser fetches these directly from Google's servers, which will see your IP address as part of that request, the same as it would for any site using Google Fonts. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for how they handle that.

## Hosting

This site is hosted on [Cloudflare Pages](https://www.cloudflare.com/). Cloudflare processes standard connection data (like your IP address) to serve the site and for security purposes, as described in [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## Search

The search on this site runs entirely in your browser against a static index — no query is ever sent to a server.

## External links

Posts link out to other sites and tools where relevant. Those are outside my control and have their own privacy policies.
